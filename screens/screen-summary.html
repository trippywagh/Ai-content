📖 Definitions

Cylinder:
A 3D solid with two parallel circular bases connected by a curved surface.
Curved Surface Area (CSA):
The area of the curved part only.
Total Surface Area (TSA):
The total area including curved surface + both circular ends.
Volume:
The amount of space a cylinder can hold inside.

💡 Tips & Tricks

💭 Think of CSA as peeling off the label of a cold drink can.
🧠 TSA = CSA + top and bottom circle areas — easy to remember!
💧 Visualize filling the cylinder with water to get volume intuitively.
📐 Use π = 22/7 or 3.14 (check what the question asks).
⚠️ Watch units: area in cm², volume in cm³.
